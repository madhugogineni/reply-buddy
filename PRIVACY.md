# Reply Buddy for X: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Reply Buddy is a browser extension that suggests short replies to posts on x.com in your own writing style, using an AI provider and API key that you choose. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes.

## The short version

- Reply Buddy has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data.
- Your API key, settings, voice and reply history are stored only in your browser.
- When you ask for reply ideas, the post's text and images are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you picked, using your key.

## What is stored, and where

All of the following is kept in your browser's extension storage on your own device. It is never sent to the developer.

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your AI provider, model and API key | To request reply ideas on your behalf |
| Your voice: style rules, sample replies and example posts | To write replies that sound like you |
| Replies you post on x.com in this browser, with the post you replied to (up to 500) | To keep learning your style |
| Your X handle | To recognise your own posts and your Replies tab |
| Settings, connect reply templates, the posting queue and a daily usage count | To run the features you turn on |

You can view and change most of this in the extension's settings, download it as a profile file with "Download my profile" (your API key is only included if you tick that box), and delete all of it by removing the extension.

## What is sent to third parties

- **Your chosen AI provider** (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, xAI, or a custom URL you enter). When you click the reply button, Reply Buddy sends the post's text, any images in it, a few nearby posts in the thread, your voice rules and some of your past replies, along with your API key. When you use "Learn my voice", it sends your recent replies to build your style rules. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to this data.
- **x.com (X).** Reply Buddy reads posts on pages you visit and, only when you choose to, fills in or posts replies and follows accounts through X's own website, as you.
- **pbs.twimg.com.** Images in posts are downloaded from X's image server so they can be sent to your AI provider. You can turn this off in settings.
- **Chrome sync (Google), only if you turn it on.** "Sync my profile across my Chrome browsers" stores your profile (settings, voice, connect replies and recent learned replies) in Chrome's built-in extension sync, tied to the Google account you're signed in to Chrome with, so your other Chrome browsers get the same profile. Your API key is only synced if you also tick "Also sync my API key". The developer never has access to this data. Google's privacy policy applies to Chrome sync.
- **ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com)**, only in versions with a paid plan: to check whether you have paid. ExtensionPay and Stripe handle any payment details. Reply Buddy never sees your card.

## What Reply Buddy does not do

- It does not send your data to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service.
- It does not sell or transfer your data, or use it for anything unrelated to suggesting and posting replies.
- It does not post, follow or reply unless you pick an option. The optional posting queue only posts replies you selected.

## Children

Reply Buddy is not directed at children under 13.
