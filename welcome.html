Welcome to Reply Buddy ⚡

Short reply ideas in your own voice, right on x.com. You always pick one and press Reply yourself. Nothing is posted for you.

1Connect an AI model

Use your own API key. It's stored only in this browser and sent only to the provider you pick. You pay the provider directly for what you use.

2Teach it your voice

Reply Buddy opens your X Replies tab, scrolls through your recent replies (about 2 minutes) and works out how you write: length, slang, punctuation, emojis. Keep that tab in front while it reads.

or

3Use it

Open your X feed. Every post now has a ⚡ next to like and repost. Click it, pick an idea, edit it if you want, then press Reply.

Every time you post a suggestion, it saves the final text and learns from it. You can change everything later in the extension's settings.
